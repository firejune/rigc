The leg chain's length — a hypothesis raised by arithmetic and refused by measurement
=====================================================================================

AUTHORING §8.1 asks for a reach check before a chain's first fit: "Take the chain's
total reach from your own rig; take the longest excursion the shot's own frames show
that chain's end travelling — and compare."

WHAT THE ARITHMETIC SAID
------------------------
This rig's leg links come from the art's own cap centroids (tools/art.ts, opaque =
alpha >= 128, cap = the opaque centroid of the topmost/bottommost 12% of opaque rows):

  front-thigh  hip cap (16.5, 8.1) -> knee cap (20.5, 102.9)      94.9 units
  front-shin   knee cap (52.3, 13.6) -> ankle cap (28.7, 169.7)  157.9 units
  front-foot   ankle cap (41.0, 5.3) -> sole                     ~60 units
                                                    hip -> sole  ~308 units

Against the frames: the pelvis sits at world y = 216.8 (the torso plate's own bottom
cap carried through `pose`'s torso placement on `idle/f0000` into the sidecar's box),
the hip 16.6 units above it at 233.4, and the floor is world y = 0 — the sidecar puts it
at image row 335.96 and this run reproduced that figure. So the STANDING leg spans about
233 units from hip to sole, with whatever knee bend the stance has, against a 308-unit
straight-leg reach.

`check` says the same thing independently, and this is the cheapest way to see it.
Pointing it at one set with a static candidate, the `in units` line reads:

  in units   candidate 352.8 x 727.9   reference 460.7 x 648.7   x0.9270     (idle, 21 frames)

727.9 against 648.7 — the candidate assembled straight from the caps is 79 units TALLER
than the reference's standing figure, which is the same 75-79 units the hip-to-sole
arithmetic predicts. ⭐ That line compares only if the shot was measured in the frames'
own units, so it is also the confirmation that it was.

THE HYPOTHESIS
--------------
A cap centroid sits at the plate's rounded TIP, while the joint two plates share sits
inside the overlap the two rounded ends make. So every link should be shorter than its
cap-to-cap distance, by roughly the cap's own radius — which on these plates is 20-40
units per link and would account for the whole 79.

THE MEASUREMENT
---------------
`LIMB_SHORTEN` (tools/rig.ts) pulls every link's far joint toward its own pivot by a
fraction, keeping the direction, and leaves the hips, the shoulders and the muzzle
alone — the first two are positions on the chest plate rather than link lengths, and the
third is the barrel. Each factor was built into its own candidate and put through the
IDENTICAL structural sweep on the identical 14-frame spread (tools/setupfit.ts, one
round), so the only difference between the rows is the factor:

  LIMB_SHORTEN   spread mean after the rotation pass   after the structural pass
  ------------   ----------------------------------   -------------------------
  0 (as read)                          6.1869                          6.2225
  0.10                                 6.8880                          6.9092
  0.18                        not run — see below
  0.25                        not run — see below

⇒ REFUSED. Shortening every link by 10% costs 0.70 on the spread mean, an 11% rise,
and the direction is unambiguous. The 0.18 and 0.25 rows were abandoned once the sign
was established, so this table reports two points and not four; the trend beyond 0.10
is NOT measured here and is not claimed.

WHAT THAT MEANS
---------------
The cap-derived link lengths are the better rig, and the 79 units are a real knee bend
in the reference's own stance rather than an error in this rig. The surgery §8.1 warns
about — "the surgery to fix it invalidates every pose already fitted with the short
chain" — was therefore not performed, and the per-frame fitting budget was not spent
twice.

⚠️ Two honest limits on this reading. The comparison is between two SEARCHES, not two
optima, so it says shortening makes this objective harder to minimise on this spread and
not that the reference's links are exactly these. And the structural sweep that produced
each row measured a basin of +-4.5 to +-9 units on every one of its 21 knobs (about +-1
to +-2 frame pixels), so the joints are weakly identified at the scale a frame can see:
what is refused here is a 10% change, and a 2% one would be inside the noise.
