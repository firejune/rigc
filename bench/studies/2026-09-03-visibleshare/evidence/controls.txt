## where each rungs bounds come from

  polish-floor      0.05 px /  0.1 deg / 0.10%  — src/pose.ts's level-0 polish floor — the step at which the pattern search stops halving and returns
  polish-floor-2x    0.1 px /  0.2 deg / 0.20%  — twice the polish floor — a pattern search that terminated at step d sits within about one step of its optimum
  pr322-scale        0.2 px /  0.4 deg / 0.40%  — sized from the band sweep so the induced anchor-residual change brackets #322's own 0.0008 — outside the convergence band, and there so the comparison against the observed 0.36 → 0.89 is direct rather than extrapolated
  readback-floor    0.16 px / 0.27 deg / 3.10%  — PS01/PS02's worst known-answer readback error, re-baselined in #306 (0.16 px / 0.27° / 3.1% scale)
  control-1px          1 px /    1 deg / 1.00%  — outside every band, on purpose — the positive control that the quantity moves at all under this knob

## the objective hand copy, verified rather than trusted

vsprobe.ts re-measures a moved placement with a hand copy of `src/pose.ts`'s own `measure`, which is
not exported. The copy is checked on every placed part of every frame by re-measuring the UNPERTURBED
reported placement and comparing against the residual the report itself carries:

  worst |mine - reported|  1.702e-5  on death@30fps/f0148 front-fist-open.png

A report rounds x and y to 3 dp and residual to 5 dp, so a disagreement of this order is the rounding
and not a second implementation. The perturbed residual is written back as the DELTA the move induced
rather than as the copy's absolute, so the copy's own offset cannot reach an eligibility test.
