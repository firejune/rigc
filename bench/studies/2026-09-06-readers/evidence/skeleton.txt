# reader probe — skeleton

invocation: bun bench/studies/2026-09-06-readers/tools/readerprobe.ts --experiment=skeleton
seed 20260906, 40,000 sweep samples, spine-core 4.3.13

Every world reader divides by `skeleton.scaleX` / `skeleton.scaleY`. That is a RUNTIME field: it is
not in skeleton data, rigc never writes it, and a consumer that mirrors a character sets it to −1.

| skeleton scale | `rotate` | `x` | `y` | `scaleX` | `scaleY` | `shearY` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (1, 1) | 37.000001445 | 6.999999234 | 11.000000000 | 1.499999950 | 0.500000017 | 12.000000272 |
| (-1, 1) | 37.000001445 | 6.999999234 | 11.000000000 | 1.499999950 | 0.500000017 | 12.000000272 |
| (1, -1) | 37.000001445 | 6.999999234 | 11.000000000 | 1.499999950 | 0.500000017 | 12.000000272 |
| (-1, -1) | 37.000001445 | 6.999999234 | 11.000000000 | 1.499999950 | 0.500000017 | 12.000000272 |
| (2, 0.5) | 37.000001445 | 6.999999234 | 11.000000000 | 1.499999950 | 0.500000017 | 12.000000272 |
| (-0.5, 3) | 37.000001445 | 6.999999234 | 11.000000000 | 1.499999950 | 0.500000017 | 12.000000272 |

⇒ **it changes nothing.** The world matrix already carries the skeleton scale and the reader divides
   the same factor back out — every reading above is identical to the digit. A candidate rule that a
   negative skeleton scale moves the producible set is REJECTED by measurement.

The one case that is not identical is a skeleton scale of ZERO, where the division has no inverse:

| skeleton scale | `rotate` | `x` | `scaleX` | `shearY` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (0, 1) | NaN | NaN | NaN | NaN |
| (1, 0) | NaN | 6.99999923423159 | NaN | NaN |
| (0, 0) | NaN | NaN | NaN | NaN |

  `NaN`, not `±Infinity`: `0/0` in the translation term and `atan2(±Infinity, ±Infinity)` in the angles.
  A `NaN` reading makes `time` NaN, and `Math.max(0, NaN)` is NaN — the animation is applied at NaN.
  Out of rigc's reach: nothing in skeleton data sets this field.
