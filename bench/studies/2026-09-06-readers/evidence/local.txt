🚨 The second table is not the first one with noise on it.
   `Slider.update` calls `bone.appliedPose.validateLocalTransform(skeleton)` before reading, and that
   recomputes the local pose from the world matrix WHEN A CONSTRAINT MOVED IT. The decomposition is
   `BonePose.set5`: `atan2Deg` for the angles — so `rotation` and `shearY` come back inside
   (−180, 180] — and `Math.sqrt` for `scaleX`, so a `scaleX` of −2 is read as +2.
