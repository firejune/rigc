## The constant every degree below passes through

  `MathUtils.PI`     = 3.1415927          (`Math.PI` = 3.141592653589793)
  `MathUtils.radDeg` = 57.29577866666166  (true 180/π = 57.29577951308232)
  a full turn converts as 2π·radDeg = 359.99999468178214
  (π − −π)·radDeg − 90 = 269.99999468178214
  (−π − π)·radDeg − 90 = -449.99999468178214

⇒ every "5.3e-6°" in this study is that constant and not a tolerance anybody chose.
